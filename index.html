About us

Our Mission & Purpose

A place where learning feels like belonging.

Ha-Barannu is a multi-purpose non-profit organization aimed to serve refugee and immigrant communities. The phrase “Ha-Barannu” is derived from Afaan Oromoo, meaning “Let’s Learn!”

Discover our programs

We believe learning should be welcoming, practical, and rooted in community. Our in-person classes and personalized tutoring meet learners where they are—building confidence, connection, and the skills to take the next step.
